# Product vision

Status: recovered planning, with unresolved choices recorded separately.

## Purpose and identity

Create Ankit's personal portfolio as an original, living digital universe. Mantis.works is a reference for experiential ambition and quality; the project must retain its own identity.

The concept is **Digital Evolution / Abstract Digital Organism**. The portfolio connects creative expression with technical depth, especially technology and AI.

Audience: clients, developers, agencies, startup founders, and general visitors. Desired reactions include “This is different,” “Feels premium,” “I want to keep scrolling,” and “How was this built?”

Personality: futuristic, creative, innovative, confident, bold, technical. Atmosphere: dark, cinematic, intelligent, organic, restrained, premium.

Repository name is MISHAI; package and page title currently use Mantis Digital Universe. Final public naming remains open.

## Central experience

One evolving world connects the chapters. Energy, particles, connections, and spatial changes gradually suggest a humanoid presence. The recognisable form is a late payoff; intermediate fragments can emerge as content develops.

The humanoid remains partly transparent, fragmented, and incomplete. Negative space and visible gaps are intentional. Avoid a solid, fully packed, static human model or a game-character presentation.

Content and visitor action give transformations a reason. The world should respond coherently, then settle. Stillness is a valid state.

## Visual direction

Recovered color direction:

| Color family                 | Intended role                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Void black / deep blue-black | Predominant environment; approximately 70–80% dark visual field |
| Electric cyan                | Data, intelligence, connection                                  |
| Deep violet                  | Evolution, transformation                                       |
| Soft white                   | Origin, focus, activation, completion                           |

These are conceptual roles, not finalized design tokens. Only the existing resting background `#05060a` is established in code. Exact accent values, contrasts, typography families, sizes, and spacing scales remain open.

Avoid excessive glow, decorative particles, camera travel, competing animations, loud HUDs, and generic cyberpunk styling. Motion and color should communicate state rather than decorate every surface.

## Product constraints

- No Education section.
- Include Work Experience.
- Development Journey is optional.
- Contact is email only; the actual address has not been supplied in the recovered content.
- Portfolio information must remain readable and accessible with keyboard, touch, and reduced motion.
- Keep the roadmap bounded. Do not introduce endless conceptual phases before implementation.

## Completion meaning

The intended product requires usable portfolio content, a coherent narrative, purposeful particle/humanoid evolution, accessible navigation, and measured device-appropriate rendering. An empty Canvas or an attractive animation alone does not complete the experience.
